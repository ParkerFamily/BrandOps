Upgrade the current AI Campaign Builder. Do NOT make it feel like a manual form.

Current problem:
The AI Campaign Builder looks too much like the manual campaign creation modal. It has too many standard input fields and does not feel AI-powered.

New direction:
The AI Campaign Builder should feel like ChatGPT for launching UGC campaigns.

Replace the current form-style AI Campaign Builder with a conversational AI campaign creation experience.

Core UX:

* One large prompt box
* User can type naturally:
  “I have a fitness app and need 20 UGC videos for TikTok ads. Budget is $5,000. I want relatable creators, strong hooks, and videos that push app installs.”
* AI extracts:

  * product/app name
  * campaign goal
  * budget
  * payout per approved video
  * creator requirements
  * video format
  * tone/style
  * platform format
  * deliverables
  * timeline
  * usage rights
  * suggested creator count

AI Builder layout:

1. Top heading:
   “Tell BrandOps what you need”

2. Subheading:
   “Describe your product, budget, audience, and goal. AI will turn it into a complete UGC campaign.”

3. Large textarea prompt:
   Placeholder:
   “Example: I’m launching a skincare brand and need 15 short UGC videos for TikTok and Reels. Budget is $3,000. I want product demos, testimonials, and strong hooks for paid ads.”

4. Quick chips under prompt:

* App installs
* Product demo
* TikTok ads
* Instagram Reels
* Testimonials
* Talking head
* Lifestyle
* Fast turnaround

Clicking chips should add context to the prompt.

5. Optional small fields ONLY:

* Total budget
* Deadline
  These should be secondary, not the main UI.

6. Button:
   “Generate Campaign”

7. AI generation state:
   Show animated steps:

* Reading campaign goal
* Building creator requirements
* Writing UGC brief
* Estimating payout strategy
* Preparing campaign draft

8. Generated campaign preview:
   After OpenAI returns, show a polished editable preview with sections:

* Campaign title
* Campaign summary
* Creator brief
* Deliverables
* Video concepts
* Hook ideas
* CTA ideas
* Suggested payout plan
* Suggested number of videos
* Usage rights language
* Timeline
* Approval criteria

9. Add actions:

* Regenerate
* Edit sections
* Save Draft
* Publish Campaign

10. Use OpenAI API:
    Use existing OpenAI setup.
    Send the full natural language prompt to OpenAI.
    Return structured JSON for the campaign draft.
    Do not hardcode fake AI output.

11. AI should infer missing details:
    If user does not provide payout per video, estimate it from total budget and target video count.
    If user does not provide video count, suggest based on budget.
    If user does not provide platforms, default to TikTok/Reels style UGC.
    If user does not provide creator type, suggest based on product and campaign goal.

12. Keep manual creation separate:
    Manual campaign creation can still exist as its own button.
    AI Campaign Builder should NOT look like the manual version.

13. Design:
    Keep current BrandOps black + neon lime theme.
    Make the modal wider and more premium.
    Use glowing borders, streaming text, subtle motion, and polished AI states.

Goal:
The user should feel like they can describe their campaign in plain English and BrandOps figures everything out.
