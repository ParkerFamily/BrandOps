Build an automated BrandOps video processing system.

When a creator uploads a video for a campaign, the app should:

1. Upload the raw video to storage.
2. Send the video audio to OpenAI Whisper/transcription.
3. Generate accurate subtitles with timestamps.
4. Use OpenAI to create short overlay text based on the campaign goal, brand name, CTA, and video transcript.
5. Add burned-in captions to the video.
6. Add a brand logo/watermark overlay.
7. Add a CTA text overlay such as “Shop Now,” “Download Now,” or the campaign CTA.
8. Export a final vertical 9:16 video ready for TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts.
9. Save the final video URL back to the campaign submission.
10. Let the creator preview the processed video before submitting it to the brand.

Use FFmpeg or Remotion for video rendering. OpenAI should only handle transcription, caption generation, and overlay text suggestions. The backend should handle the actual video editing/rendering.

I would NEVER automatically submit the AI-edited version to the brand without the creator seeing it first.

Think about it:

Captions could be wrong
CTA could cover their face
Logo could be too big
Timing could look weird

Creators will want final control.

For Replit I'd say:

After video processing is complete, generate a preview version that is visible only to the creator who uploaded the content.

The creator must be able to:

- Watch the processed video
- Compare original vs processed version
- Toggle captions on/off
- Toggle logo overlay on/off
- Toggle CTA overlay on/off
- Approve final version

Only after approval should the processed video be submitted to the brand for review.
