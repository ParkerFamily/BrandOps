Update the BrandOps creator discovery and campaign matching logic based on the new product direction.

Do NOT redesign the whole app. Refine the current web SaaS build.

Core product clarification:
BrandOps is NOT mainly an influencer-posting marketplace.
BrandOps is for brands to hire creators to produce UGC videos that the BRAND will post/run on the brand’s own social pages and ads.

Because of this:

* Creator follower count should NOT be the main metric.
* Creator engagement rate should NOT be the main metric.
* Creator niche should NOT be hard-coded as the main identity.
* The brand’s campaign defines what type of creator/content is needed.

Update Creator cards to focus on UGC production value:

Show:

* AI Match %
* Brand rating
* Completed campaigns
* On-time delivery %
* Average turnaround time
* Approval rate
* Revision rate
* Suggested payout
* Content style strengths:

  * Talking head
  * Product demo
  * Lifestyle
  * Voiceover
  * Testimonial
  * Comedy
  * Tutorial

Remove/demote:

* follower count
* engagement rate
* average views
* fixed niche labels like Beauty, Fitness, Fashion as primary identity

Social platform icons can stay, but only as “content format experience,” not as audience size proof.

Campaign matching logic:
When a brand creates a campaign, the campaign should define what matters:

* creator type
* age range
* gender/presentation if relevant
* video style
* tone
* product category
* platform format
* deadline
* budget
* payout per approved video
* number of videos needed

Then BrandOps calculates AI Match based on:

* similar completed campaign history
* approval rate
* delivery speed
* brand ratings
* content style fit
* revision history
* campaign requirements

Creator cards should show “Why matched” instead of fixed niche identity.

Examples:

* Strong product demo history
* 96% approval rate
* Delivers within 48 hours
* Similar campaign experience
* Low revision rate
* Strong talking-head performance

Budget / payout logic:
Brands set a total campaign budget first.
Then brands can assign payout amounts to individual creators within that total budget.

Example:
Total campaign budget: $5,000

Creator payouts:

* Creator A: $100/video
* Creator B: $150/video
* Creator C: $250/video
* Creator D: $75/video

Budget should update live as creators are assigned and videos are approved.

Add UI for:

* total campaign budget
* remaining budget
* payout per approved video
* custom payout per creator
* approved payout queue
* estimated remaining videos based on budget

Important payment rule:
Money should only become payable after brand approves a submitted video.

Flow:
Campaign created →
Creators invited/apply →
Brand assigns or confirms payout →
Creator submits video →
Brand approves →
Payout moves to approved queue →
Stripe payout can be triggered.
