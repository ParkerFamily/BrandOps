────────────────────────────────────────
1. BUG FIX — SET UP PAYOUTS (Guideline 2.1a)
────────────────────────────────────────

Issue on build 27: Tapping "Set up payouts" showed "Could not open payouts" for new creator accounts.

Fix: New creators without a payout account now open Stripe Connect onboarding directly in the in-app browser. The app no longer falls back to the BrandOps web dashboard settings page (which could be mistaken for subscription access).

How to test payout setup (creator account):
1. Sign in (Sign in with Apple, Google, or email).
2. Profile tab → tap "Set up payouts" OR Settings → Payout account & history → "Set up payout account".
3. A secure Stripe-hosted onboarding form opens in the in-app browser.
4. Uploading campaigns remains free; payout setup is optional and only for receiving compensation after approved work.

Deletion permanently removes the Firebase Authentication account and Firestore user profile. A screen recording of this flow is attached.

3. IN-APP PURCHASES / SUBSCRIPTIONS (Guideline 3.1.1)
────────────────────────────────────────

BrandOps is a UGC marketplace. The iOS app is FREE and does NOT sell digital content, subscriptions, premium features, or in-app purchases.

CREATORS:
- Join campaigns, upload video submissions, and use all app features at no cost.
- Optional payout account setup is marketplace compensation infrastructure (receive pay for approved work). It is NOT purchasing digital goods, app unlocks, or subscription plans.
- The iOS app does not read, restore, or gate features behind externally purchased subscription plans.

BRANDS:
- The iOS app is limited to reviewing creator submissions for existing campaigns.
- Campaign creation, team management, and any business subscription/billing exist ONLY on our separate web dashboard (brandopsapp.com) in Safari — not inside the iOS app.
- The iOS app does not display subscription plans, upgrade prompts, billing screens, or "restore purchases" flows.

There are no IAP products in this app because nothing is sold in the iOS app.
